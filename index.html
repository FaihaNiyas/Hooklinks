How long does SEO take to show results?

Most businesses see early movement within the first 4 to 8 weeks, with steady growth in rankings and traffic over 3 to 6 months. Paid ads can bring enquiries from the first week.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

No long contracts. Our monthly packages let you start small, then scale up or down as your business grows.

Which areas do you work with?

We are based in Ferryhill, County Durham, and work with businesses right across the UK, online and over the phone.

What do I get in the free audit?

A clear look at your website and online presence: an SEO health check, speed and mobile review, a quick competitor snapshot and three practical quick wins you can act on.

Is ad spend included in the package price?

Ad spend is paid directly to Google, Meta or TikTok and sits separately from our management fee. We agree a budget with you before anything goes live.

How will I know it is working?

You get a clear monthly report showing traffic, leads, ad results and what we are doing next. No jargon, and you can call us any time.

Can you work with my existing website?

Yes. We can improve your current site for speed, SEO and conversions, or build you a new one if that is the better option.
